The name

The word means blood seal.

Keppan (血判) is the blood-oath seal signed to enter a classical Japanese school — the oldest membership commitment there is.

Etymology

血判

血 (chi) — blood
判 (han) — seal, judgment

In the old Japanese schools — the koryū — you didn't sign up. You petitioned. And if the school accepted you, you signed the enrollment oath and sealed it with your own blood. That seal was called keppan (血判).

It meant the school's knowledge would be protected. It meant the student belonged to a lineage now, and the lineage belonged to them. It was the deepest membership commitment in martial arts history — maybe in any history.

We didn't invent this name. We found it.

Keppan the company builds the systems that memberships run on — payments, schedules, attendance, the relationship itself. We launched first in martial arts because that's where we come from, and because it's the hardest membership business there is: nobody stays at a martial arts school out of convenience. They stay out of commitment.

"In the old schools, joining meant signing in blood. Keppan is how your members commit now."

No blood required. The seriousness stays.

Before Keppan

We watched the same problems break the same schools, year after year.

Bobby Freda built Ground Standard into one of the most recognized agencies in the martial arts industry — working with academies from New Jersey to the West Coast on marketing, operations, advertising, and growth. Over more than a decade, the team went deep inside hundreds of schools.

The technical problems were almost always the same: billing that required manual intervention, scheduling systems that didn't talk to attendance, rank tracking done in spreadsheets, communications sent from personal email accounts. Owners who were excellent instructors were drowning in administration because the software they had wasn't built for them.

Every platform on the market was either too generic, too expensive, or built for a different kind of business entirely. The martial arts school — with its belt system, its family accounts, its high-frequency scheduling and deep retention cycles — was an afterthought. That's why Keppan exists.

The Ground Standard ecosystem

Built over a decade. Launched for you now.

2013

Ground Standard founded

Bobby Freda launched Ground Standard in Red Bank, New Jersey to solve a problem he kept seeing firsthand: martial arts schools with great instructors and weak back-office systems. Starting locally, he built a team to handle marketing, communications, and advertising for academy owners who were spending too much time on administration and not enough time on the mat.

2016

Gear brand launched

The ecosystem expanded with a martial arts gear brand — the first product line to extend Ground Standard's mission beyond services and into the school community itself.

2018

Fractional Operations Program

Ground Standard introduced the Fractional Operations Program — the only program of its kind in the martial arts industry. For the first time, academy owners could access full marketing, communications, advertising, staff training, and operational infrastructure at a fraction of the cost of building it in-house. The program scaled nationally, and the client list grew from local to coast-to-coast.

2025

Try A Martial Art + After Hours Podcast

Two new channels reached the broader community: Try A Martial Art, a discovery platform connecting prospective students with schools, and the After Hours Podcast, covering the business side of running a martial arts school with honesty and without the usual marketing fluff.

2026

Keppan + Skool Community

After years of working inside hundreds of schools across every platform on the market — and watching the same limitations surface again and again — Keppan launched. The software that school owners should have had from the start. Alongside it, the Ground Standard Skool community brought academy owners together to share what's actually working. The ecosystem was complete.

The schools Keppan is built for: high-attendance, high-commitment academies where the belt system, the community, and the operations all have to work together.

The founder

Keppan was founded by Bobby Freda — founder of Ground Standard, the agency that has served hundreds of martial arts schools across the country since 2013 — in Red Bank, New Jersey.

The commitment principle behind Keppan isn't a brand decision. It's the operating reality of every school Bobby has worked with: the schools that survive — and grow into institutions — are the ones where members stay because they're committed to something, not just because they haven't gotten around to canceling.

The Fractional Operations Program, the gear brand, Try A Martial Art, the podcast, the community — every piece of the Ground Standard ecosystem was built to solve a real problem Bobby observed working directly inside martial arts schools. Keppan is the software chapter of that same story.

Built to support commitment. Not just process transactions.
