Attendance

Check-in in seconds.

Kiosk check-in at the door, live rosters during class, and the full attendance picture for every student — without manual entry.

app.keppan.com / check-in

What's included

  • Front-desk kiosk check-in — tap a name, done in under two seconds
  • Live class rosters visible from any device during class
  • Full attendance history per student, filterable by date and class
  • Attendance streaks that keep students motivated to show up
  • Absence alerts — know when an active member hasn't been in
  • Check-in by QR code, PIN, or name search
  • Class capacity tracking with waitlist support
  • Attendance reports by class, instructor, or time period

Know who's showing up

Attendance is the earliest signal retention sends.

A student who drops from three classes a week to one is telling you something before they ever think about canceling. Keppan's absence alerts surface that pattern early — so you can reach out before the decision is made.

Instructors pull up the live class roster on any phone or tablet from the mat. No clipboard, no paper, no front-desk trip. After class, it's already in the member's history.
